Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains what PIXORA collects and why. Browsing wallpapers does not require an account.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address and password when you sign up. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider (Supabase); we do not see them in plain text.
  • Profile data: username, display name, bio and avatar, which are visible to other people.
  • Uploads: wallpapers and videos you submit, with their titles, categories and tags.
  • Social activity: who you follow, who you block, and reports you submit.
  • Messages: text, voice, files, reactions and polls you send, plus read status and typing and last-seen indicators.
  • On your device: your liked wallpapers, theme choice and sign-in session are kept in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser data removes them.

How we use it

To run the site: show content, sign you in, deliver messages, review uploads, handle reports and keep PIXORA safe.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase stores the database and files (project hosted in London, UK).
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • jsDelivr delivers a code library the site needs.

These providers receive technical data such as your IP address when you load the site.

Advertising and analytics

PIXORA does not currently show ads or run analytics tools. If we add advertising in future, such as Google AdSense, we will update this policy first and explain the cookies involved and how to opt out.

Who can see your data

Profiles and approved uploads are public. Messages are visible to the people in the conversation. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted, so site administrators with database access could technically read them; we only do so to handle reports or fix problems.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep account data while your account exists. To delete your account and the data tied to it, email us at YOUR_CONTACT_EMAIL.

Children

PIXORA is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

We will post changes on this page with a new date. Questions: Contact.
