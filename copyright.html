Copyright & Reporting

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Our approach

People who upload to PIXORA must own the content or have permission to share it. Uploads are reviewed by an administrator before they are published. We remove content that infringes someone's rights, and we may suspend accounts that repeatedly upload infringing or harmful content.

Report copyright infringement

If you believe something on PIXORA infringes your copyright, email us at YOUR_CONTACT_EMAIL with:

  • The page address or title of the wallpaper or video.
  • A description of your original work, and proof that you own it or act for the owner.
  • Your name and contact details.
  • A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorised.

We review each complaint and remove content that infringes.

Report inappropriate content or users

Signed-in users can report a user or a message from the app. You can also email us. We review reports and act on those that break our rules.

Content you upload

You keep ownership of what you upload. By uploading, you confirm you have the right to share it on PIXORA and allow PIXORA to display it and offer it for download.
