About PIXORA

A place to find wallpapers and short videos for your phone and screen.

PIXORA is a wallpaper discovery website. You can browse wallpapers by category, search by keyword, preview an image full screen, like the ones you enjoy, and download them for your lock screen or home screen. Short videos are available too.

What you can do

  • Browse categories such as Anime, Nature, Space, Cars, Technology, AMOLED and more.
  • Search wallpapers and videos by title or tag.
  • Create a free account to follow creators, send messages and upload your own work.
  • Pick a random wallpaper when you can't decide.

Creators

Registered users can upload wallpapers and videos and build a creator profile. Every upload is reviewed by a PIXORA administrator before it appears publicly.

Copyright and moderation

Only upload work you created or have permission to share. We remove content that infringes copyright or breaks our rules. See Copyright & Reporting to report content, and our Privacy Policy to see how account data is handled.

Who runs PIXORA

PIXORA is an independent project run by its owner. You can reach us on the Contact page.
